nych/data/dictionary_words.txt.gz
=================================

What it is: the lowercase entries (91,034 words, including inflected forms,
hyphenated words, and contractions) of the English Speller Database (ESDB,
formerly SCOWL), size 60, American + British spellings, max variant level 1,
accents removed. Capitalized-only entries (people's names, places, months)
are deliberately excluded: nych.protected_terms uses this list to decide
whether a word "has a dictionary definition", and a name that appears only
in capitalized form must not count as one.

Source: https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist
Commit: 1e5b7d3a72f47a71da5d28686c1dd4b397178485
Generated with:
    make
    ./scowl --db scowl.db word-list 60 A,B,Z 1 --deaccent \
      | grep -E "^[a-z][a-z'-]*$" | LC_ALL=C sort -u | gzip -9 -n

Size 60 is at or below 80, so the UKACD terms do not apply; American and
British spellings without the Australian 'D' spelling / 'AU' region, so the
AU terms do not apply. The applicable notice follows.
